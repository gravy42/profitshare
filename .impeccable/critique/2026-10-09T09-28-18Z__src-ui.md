## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** authored for this product in substance, interchangeable in chrome. The content layer could belong to nothing else: Day int / Night int strip paper, "End of Day N · 4 4/8 pgs · 4 cast", a DOOD with W/H cells, "Every tier, both ways", "the Sing Sing deal" as a radio label, copy that explains a number before it asks for one. The shell is generic: plum-to-teal gradient header, Helvetica Neue, 14px rounded cards, uppercase 13px purple panel titles, ghost toolbar buttons. Swap the words and it is any 2024 React admin. The Stripboard is the one surface where the visual language is grounded in the domain; Start and Deal borrow the app's generic card system.

**Deterministic scan:** `impeccable detect src` exits 2 with 2 findings, both `border-accent-on-rounded` in styles.css (101 `.strip.dragover`, 200 `.dood-full tr.dragover td`). Both are false positives: transient drag-over drop indicators, not card accents. `src/ui` alone is clean. The in-page detector (detect.js injected over the production build, preflight passed in all 9 runs) reported Start 19 / 11 / 20, Deal 42 / 24 / 43, Stripboard 46 / 38 / 20 findings (light 1440 / dark 1440 / light 390). Almost all of it is one family, contrast, which the design review had also flagged, and which the measurements make concrete (below). The other families: ai-color-palette (one brand token, --plum, flagged once per heading: cosmetic), nested-cards on Start (the drop zone inside a card: arguable), tiny-text (the 10px `.ctl .hint` on Deal and the 11px strip synopses), line-length (help paragraphs run 145 to 149 characters per line at 1440), and at 390 real overflow: `header.top` 156px past the right edge on Deal and 184px on the Stripboard, and the three stat values overflowing their boxes by 21px.

**Visual overlays:** none shown to you; the detector ran headless in Playwright because no browser in this session can reach the container's localhost. Console output was captured in full instead.

Where the two assessments agree: contrast, the phone header, the day grip, the Deal tab's hint sizes. Where the review caught what the detector cannot: the sample arriving without day breaks, silent repricing, the arming bug on Start, the matrix having no keyboard path.

## Overall Impression

The product knows exactly what it is and says so in every sentence; the screen does not yet match the writing. A filmmaker lands on Deal and gets the best moment in the app, three cards and a matrix that answer her three questions, then has to find it under two yellow boxes and 2,000px of equal-weight prose. She opens the Stripboard and the first schedule she ever sees is "1 DAYS · heavy". The single biggest opportunity is the same move on both screens: put the answer first and make the high-stakes actions say what they just did.

## What's Working

1. **The tier matrix.** Sixteen full recomputes of the raw budget, each cell showing the raise, fit or over, and headroom, with the current position outlined. The product thesis rendered as one table.
2. **Copy that explains before it asks.** "Hourly rates don't change, so the budget gets cheaper by exactly the overtime you're not paying"; "The floor isn't zero because a worked day at $0 is a wage-law problem for a W-2 crew member"; the .mbd rejection that names the Movie Magic menu to use. No budgeting tool talks like this.
3. **The board-replacement safety net.** Merge/Replace notice in plain words, a confirm that counts what goes, a backup that survives reload, a Restore bar. A wrong click is not the end of it, and the UI says so.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] The sample board opens as "1 DAYS · heavy" on a "12-day feature."**
Why it matters: it is the first thing she sees on the Stripboard, it contradicts the flash she just read, and the DOOD, the Days table and the cast-day flow all look broken. A new user cannot tell the tool works.
Fix: ship SALT FLAT with its 12 day breaks placed (re-pin the engine totals), or make the empty state explicit: a callout above the strips, "No day breaks yet. Fit to 12 days places them; drag them after," with Fit as the primary button.
Suggested command: `/impeccable onboard`

**[P1] Contrast fails across both themes.**
Why it matters: production paperwork is read at 11px on set and in dark rooms; the measured pairs fail AA by a wide margin. Light: `.hint` 3.8:1 at 10px, `.muted` 3.0 to 3.8:1 on the strips and table labels, `.sub` on the stat cards 3.8:1, the day-lift grip white on #f6f2ea at 1.1:1 (invisible, the only way to move a whole day). Dark: every panel heading 1.7:1, `.help` 2.3:1 (hard-coded with no dark override), strip synopses 2.2:1 because the strips keep light paper while `--muted` goes dark. Both: the "12 hr" primary button 2.7:1.
Fix: one pass on tokens. Darken `--muted` in light to about #6b655a (4.6:1 on white); give `.help` a dark-mode value; give headings a lighter plum in dark; set `color: var(--ink)` on the day grip; use a darker orange or dark text on `.btn.primary`; give strips their own `--strip-muted`.
Suggested command: `/impeccable polish`

**[P1] Fit to N days, Clear day breaks and "gone: delete the lines" reprice the film with no acknowledgement.**
Why it matters: PRODUCT.md principle 1 says anything that changes what the budget reads should say so. These three move Budget to raise by tens of thousands (Fit to 12 moved it $18,194 in the run; "gone" dropped $71,500) and the only sign is a header number changing. She notices when an investor PDF is wrong.
Fix: after each, flash the delta in the board-restore voice: "Fit to 12 days: 11 day breaks placed, cast days recounted, Budget to raise +$18,194. Undo brings the old board back." Give "gone: delete the lines" a confirm that counts lines and dollars, as Replace already does.
Suggested command: `/impeccable harden`

**[P2] The phone layout breaks at the header.**
Why it matters: PRODUCT.md says mobile reading is expected. At 390 the page scrolls sideways (header 156px past the edge on Deal, 184px on the Stripboard), the three stat values overflow their boxes, the strip's set column collapses to 0px so the location vanishes, and the glyph controls have no tooltips on touch.
Fix: wrap the header toolbar under the title at narrow widths, let stat values shrink or wrap, give `.strip` a two-row grid under 700px with the set first.
Suggested command: `/impeccable adapt`

**[P2] The Deal tab buries the decision; the Stripboard hides its controls.**
Why it matters: she arrives on Deal to learn whether the film fits at her tier and what to change if not; the matrix answers it 950px down under two notices that both say "change any of these terms." On the Stripboard, ⌄ ⏎ ½ ⊕ ⋮⋮ carry the whole interaction and are explained only by a paragraph she re-reads every visit.
Fix: one notice, matrix directly under the stat cards, the prep/wrap/post and financing panels behind `details` until the pay model is set; label the two main strip actions on hover/focus, move ½ and ⊕ into the open tagger, collapse the help into a disclosure that remembers its state.
Suggested commands: `/impeccable distill` (Deal), `/impeccable clarify` (Stripboard)

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (first-timer):** the header reads "SALT FLAT · $787,266 · LBA" before she has clicked anything on Start, so "Three ways in" reads as a lie or a lost file. "LBA" and "MLB" never expanded. After "Start from scratch" all four buttons turn orange and say "Confirm:", so she cannot tell which she pressed. Nothing says "saved"; the footer's "until you press Save" reads as "unsaved."

**Sam (keyboard / screen reader):** matrix cells are `<td onClick>` with no tabindex, role or key handler, so the matrix is mouse-only. Strips are draggable divs with no keyboard reorder and no live region. The ⌄ button's accessible name is "⌄ 2". Fit, Clear and Delete announce nothing. The contrast failures above are hers first.

**Riley (stress tester):** selecting the "No script text on this strip" placeholder offers to tag it as a Prop. Shoot days can read 12 while the board has one day and nothing says they disagree. Arming with "Open .json" then clicking "Load the sample" replaces the project. Day labels vanish silently on Clear day breaks.

**Cris at night with a Shamel export (from PRODUCT.md):** she drops the .xlsx, lands on Deal, and the import report ("154 lines, 6 weekly wage lines rewritten as days, 12 memo rows folded") is a six-second flash she cannot get back; the one thing she needs to trust, that everything came in to the cent, is gone before she finishes reading. She scrolls past prose to find whether she fits her tier. She drops the script next and gets a board with no day breaks, and the one button that would give her a schedule is one of four look-alikes with no hint that it also rewrites the cast days on the budget she just imported.

## Minor Observations

- `.tag.points` in dark mode is #b58ee0 on a hard-coded near-white; chips lose contrast on the top sheet and About too.
- Sticky `thead th` is global; on the phone capture the matrix and ATL table headers rendered mid-table.
- `header.top` is 206px tall at 390 before the tabs; the KPI row wraps unevenly.
- "Shooting day" 10 hr / 12 hr is the only orange-filled control on Deal, so it reads as the primary action.
- "Pages / day" resets on every keystroke; typing 5.5 passes through 5.
- The Stripboard title's numbers (26 scenes · 69 6/8 pages · 1 days) are the same 13px uppercase as every panel title.
- Strip `title={s.synopsis}` duplicates the synopsis already visible beneath the set.
- Start's From-scratch card orders Title, Shoot days, Chart of accounts; Deal orders Title, Version, Shoot days; Version is missing from Start.
- Help paragraphs run 145 to 149 characters per line at 1440 (`.help` max-width 820px at 12.5px).
- Undo has no shortcut and no tooltip naming what it would undo.
- Dark mode keeps strips on light paper (the convention) but nothing bridges it: a bright block in a dark page, with the tagger's script pane dark again beneath.

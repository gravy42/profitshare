## Priority Issues

**[P1] Hand edits to the board reprice the film silently, and the stale notice contradicts the state.**
Why it matters: dragging a strip across a day bar or clicking a day-bar × changes cast days and the header "Budget to raise" with no message, while the previous sticky notice ("Auto day breaks: …") stays on screen. The one place the product's promise is demonstrated by hand, it is demonstrated silently, and a wrong on-screen message is worse than none.
Fix: route `removeStrip` on a day bar, `moveStrips`, `moveDay`, `insertDayBreak` and `splitStrip` through `reschedule()` so every board change reports breaks and delta with the undo hint; clear the sticky message on any later project change; make the message a live region.
Suggested command: `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] The SAG "over" stat has no next step at the moment of highest anxiety.**
Why it matters: "over by $105,460 of $700,000" in red is the headline of the Deal tab for the sample; the way out is in the matrix one row down or in the help prose, neither linked. The primary user, alone at night, reads "over" as "illegal".
Fix: give the bad stat an action line: "Qualifies for Low Budget as drawn (cap $2,000,000)" with a "Set target to Low Budget" button, "or cut $105,460 of total production cost"; label the matrix's current cell "your film" instead of a 1px border.
Suggested command: `/impeccable clarify`

**[P1] Strip actions are glyph-only at rest; the tag menu is an 18-item wall.**
Why it matters: ⌄ ⏎ ½ with the words hidden until hover means recognition fails on every strip for a first-timer and there is no hover on touch; the tag menu then presents 18 ungrouped categories after a text selection.
Fix: show the word on every strip at 1440 (the grid already has `auto auto` columns), glyph-only below 760; group the tag menu into four rows (People, Things, Set, Post / Other) with the categories this board uses first.
Suggested command: `/impeccable distill`

**[P2] The Deal tab has no hierarchy between the answer, the levers and the explanation; the ATL table breaks at 390.**
Why it matters: three near-identical dollar figures in the first 300px, five equal-weight panels, eleven always-open help paragraphs; at phone width the ATL money table wraps one character per line ("P r o d u c e r").
Fix: one answer at display size, the matrix headed "Where the film could sit", the term panels as a stepped form with help behind a "why" toggle; the ATL table scrolls sideways like the matrix instead of breaking words.
Suggested command: `/impeccable layout`

**[P2] Dark mode: unchecked strip checkboxes render dark on the light strips; day-bar label placeholders are 2.8:1.**
Why it matters: every strip looks pre-selected, and "picked" is the gateway to block moves; the placeholder "label" on eleven day bars fails AA.
Fix: `.strip .pick { color-scheme: light }` (or draw the box with the strip's own ink); lighten the day-bar placeholder to rgba(255,255,255,.6).
Suggested command: `/impeccable polish`

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex (power user):** no keyboard way to reorder a strip or drop a day break; no ⌘S, Save is a ghost button among seven; a click per word per category to tag; Pages / day commits on blur, which Alex won't notice; Shoot days lives in three places.

**Sam (keyboard / screen reader):** matrix cells are exemplary. Strips are draggable divs with no keyboard path and the strip checkbox has no accessible name; strip colour is colour-only with TOD as 11px muted text; the reschedule notice is not a live region, so it is never announced.

**Jordan (first-timer):** "Three ways in" above four buttons; "Chart of accounts: Standard feature (274 accounts)" is the first undefined dropdown; ⌄ ⏎ ½ undefined until hover; nothing says which of Auto day breaks and Fit to N days to press first once breaks exist; the arming notice still names the first button after the arm moves to another.

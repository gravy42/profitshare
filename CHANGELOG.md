# Changelog

ProfitShare is built in the open by Cris Graves, with Claude doing the typing, while she budgets and schedules her first feature. This log is kept by day so anyone picking the project up can see what changed and why. Newest day first. Commit hashes are on `main`.

The app is local-first: your project lives in your browser and in the `.profitshare.json` files you download. Nothing here changes a saved file without saying so.

## 2026-10-22

- **The deck shows the whole stack, and the photos survive the PDF.** The ask slide now reads the financing the way an investor needs it: the whole budget, then where it comes from (fiscal sponsorship and grants that are never repaid, the California credit net of audit, sale and bridge with the application round it fits, the federal credit marked as a bill not yet law, any local perks) and last the investors' slice with its terms, plus one sentence saying what the investor ask becomes if the credits or the sponsorship do not land, so a small ask never reads as the whole raise. `fundingStack()` in `src/engine/deck.ts`. The numbers slide says "after $1,000,000 in sponsorship and grants and $677,358 in credits". Three fixes behind it: the deck (and the AI's brief of the film) applied the deal a second time to a project that already had it, so the budget on the slides ran about $46K over what every other tab showed; "Investors put in" on the Pitch tab, the deck and the cases now all come from the same figures. *Download the PDF* lost every photograph in Safari (the look slides came out as headings over midnight), so the pictures no longer go through the slide capture: each slide is captured with its pictures hidden and the pictures are cropped as the layout crops them and placed by the PDF library at the spot the layout gave them, as JPEGs, which also makes the file smaller. Research slides fit: a finding over 260 characters shares its slide with one other and keeps to its column (long ones were running off the page), the source shows as its domain, and "Primary: …" in the Primary box no longer says the label twice. The split layout is the default now, and in it the title, the case and the team take the pictures while the data slides (audience, comps, plan, numbers, deal, research, the ask) keep the whole page; a picture a look book holds twice shows once. ## 2026-10-21

- **Fixed points.** A participant's points can be a block set by the deal instead of days × tier × bonus: "fix" next to the bonus on the Points schedule turns the Points cell into a number you type, "fixed" turns it back. It's for the people whose share is bought with money rather than days, a name actor who traded a premium for points or a producer whose fee went to the back end, so a two-day role can hold a real share without a bonus of 59×. Everyone else still earns points by days. Tests: 83.
- **The crew bump leaves the director alone.** The As-budgeted "+N%" on the Deal tab raises crew wage lines; it no longer touches wage lines in the above-the-line accounts (1100 story, 1200 producers, 1300 director), because a director's or producer's fee is a deal, not a crew rate. The crew-bump test now checks it. Tests: 82.
- **The Alone Girl Universe look.** ProfitShare now draws from Cris's design system (the brand book for *Alone Girl*, Blissful Spinster, the reels and the decks). Two themes from its tokens: **Night** is the film's own, midnight ground with periwinkle type, the way the pitch deck reads; **Day** is cream paper and midnight ink for daylight and print. The masthead is always Night, with the title typed and a marigold string under it. Type is Work Sans for everything you read and Special Elite (open-licensed, standing in for the deck's Adler) for the title, the script text and the sign-off. The old plum / teal / orange semantic colours map onto the palette: cobalt and periwinkle where purple meant something (strips, chips, the matrix), sienna and tangerine for deferred, marigold for the accent, brick for over budget. Corners square off; only the keys are round.
- **Buttons are typewriter keys.** Every button is a key from the blue typewriter on the deck cover: a cream cap tilted toward you so the long edge is on top, a dish in the surface, a dark rim and a stem that lifts on hover and presses flat on click. Primary actions are the one key somebody painted marigold; the masthead's keys take the typewriter's slate body. Labels are uppercase here and only here, because that is what is printed on a key. The × on a row stays a plain ×.
- **Night / Day / System switch** in the masthead (three keys; the chosen one is the painted, pressed one). The choice is kept in the browser (`profitshare.theme`) and applied before the page paints, so there is no flash; System removes the override and follows the OS, as before. The smoke test still drives both themes through the OS preference.
- Fonts come in as npm packages (`@fontsource/work-sans`, `@fontsource/special-elite`, both OFL) and are inlined in the single-file build. Tests: 82.

## 2026-10-08

- **Incentives: California law changes.** SB 186 (signed Sept 19, 2026) changed the Program 4.0 refund election from 90% over five years to 95% over two; the model and the tab's labels follow. From tax year 2027 a credit bought from an independent film is exempt from the buyer's $5M cap, noted on the "sold at" hint. AB 2319's standalone post-production credit is documented in the source and on the tab but not modelled: it is for pictures that did not take the production credit, needs at least 75% or $1M of editorial post in California (whichever is greater), and a film in Program 4.0 already has its post inside the production credit. (7f63f58)
- **Calendar: All / One / Three months.** The full range as a grid (as before), one month at full width, or three side by side, with earlier/later paging. The choice is remembered in the browser. (6819067)

## 2026-10-07

- **Deal: crew rates over budget.** The As-budgeted pay model gains a percent: every crew wage line (payroll, not a SAG performer, not an ATL premium) at its budgeted rate plus N%, so the budget's own ladder survives. The "Every tier, both ways" matrix now shows As budgeted · Budget +10% · Budget +15% · Everyone at scale for each SAG tier; click a cell to apply, or type any percent under the As-budgeted radio. This is the hybrid between a plain budget and the Sing Sing model: everyone paid a little better than the budget says, and everyone on the back end. (0c3f96b)

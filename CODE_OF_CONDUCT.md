# Code of conduct

ProfitShare is made for filmmakers, many of them budgeting a film for the first time. This is how we treat each other in its issues, pull requests and anywhere else the project is discussed.

## What we ask

- **Be kind.** Assume the other person means well and is doing their best.
- **Be patient with beginners.** Nobody here has to know budgeting, film or code already. A question that seems basic is welcome.
- **Talk about the work, not the person.** Say what is wrong with a number, a screen or a line of code, and what would make it better.
- **Take no for an answer.** This is a small project with a clear purpose. Not every idea will fit, and a declined idea is not a judgement of the person who had it.
- **Keep private things private.** Do not post anyone's budget, contract, pay, contact details or other personal information, your own collaborators' included.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, mockery, or comments that demean someone for who they are: their sex, gender, sexuality, race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, age, disability, body, or level of experience.
- Harassment of any kind, sexual attention nobody asked for, threats, or following someone from place to place to keep an argument going.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Spam and advertising that has nothing to do with the project.

## If something goes wrong

Use GitHub's own tools: the **Report content** option in the menu beside any comment, issue or pull request, or GitHub's report abuse form for an account. Reports made that way are private and go to GitHub's own staff. You can also block an account from your own, so it can no longer reach you.

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and contributions that break this code, and may block a person who keeps breaking it. This is a one-person project, so these calls are made by one person, as fairly and as soon as she can.

## Where this applies

In this repository, and in any other place where someone is speaking for or about the project.

// CUE 1: title card
ProfitShare. Open-source budgeting and profit-share modelling for independent film. Nothing leaves your browser.

[PAUSE: 1.5s]

// CUE 2: start screen
Three ways in. Start from scratch, drop in a budget you already have, or load the sample.

[PAUSE: 1.5s]

// CUE 3: deal tab
Everything about the deal lives on one page, and it sits on top of your budget instead of rewriting it. Salt Flat, our sample, is a twelve-day SAG feature with a budget of eight hundred and twelve thousand dollars, as budgeted.

[PAUSE: 1.5s]

// CUE 4: everyone at scale
Flip to the Sing Sing deal. One hourly rate for everyone, above and below the line, overtime on top. The budget reprices while you watch.

[PAUSE: 1.5s]

// CUE 5: premiums
Producer fees, the script, the star allowance. Each one can stay up front, move to points, or come off the budget entirely.

[PAUSE: 1.5s]

// CUE 6: floor rate
Prep, wrap and post days at a floor rate, with the balance on the back end. Days worked don't change, so nobody's points change. What they're paid up front does.

[PAUSE: 1.5s]

// CUE 7: top sheet
The top sheet shows the result. Lines the deal set are marked. Days and descriptions are still yours.

[PAUSE: 1.5s]

// CUE 8: SAG
SAG sets your tier by total production cost. Your profit-share points aren't part of that cost, which is what makes this work. This page checks the budget against every tier.

[PAUSE: 1.5s]

// CUE 9: points
The back end splits by days worked times a tier multiplier, at three revenue scenarios. Grants are never recouped. Everyone on the schedule is here, wages next to their points.

[PAUSE: 1.5s]

// CUE 10: stripboard
The stripboard offers ease, flexibility and integration with your budget. Drag and reorder your days, adjust the number of shoot days, adjust the number of pages per day, then push cast days into the budget and the points schedule follows.

[PAUSE: 1.5s]

// CUE 11: drop import
Bring your own. Drop a Shamel export, a Movie Magic export, a Movie Magic board, or a script from Final Draft, Highland, Celtx or a PDF anywhere on the page. It works out what it got.

[PAUSE: 1.5s]

// CUE 12: end card
ProfitShare. Free, open source, and yours. github dot com slash gravy four two slash profitshare.
